### Engagement Strategy:

1. **Post Timing:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday: 9 AM or 5 PM
   - Best days: Tuesday and Wednesday
   - Avoid weekends for professional content

2. **Hashtag Strategy:**
   - Use 5-8 relevant hashtags
   - Mix popular and niche tags
   - Create a branded hashtag (e.g., #AbelBuilds)

3. **Engagement Actions:**
   - Comment on posts from tech companies you admire
   - Share insights on web development trends
   - Engage with other developers' content
   - Join relevant LinkedIn groups

4. **Content Calendar:**
   - Week 1: Portfolio launch
   - Week 2: Technical breakdown
   - Week 3: Project spotlight (CampusHub)
   - Week 4: Skills showcase
   - Week 5: Learning journey
   - Week 6: Future goals/seeking opportunities
